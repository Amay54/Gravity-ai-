# Corporate Intelligence Dossier: NVIDIA Corporation (NASDAQ: NVDA)

## Executive Summary
NVIDIA Corporation pioneered GPU computing and artificial intelligence infrastructure. They design high-performance graphics processing units and AI software architectures (CUDA).

- **HQ**: Santa Clara, California, USA
- **Valuation**: $3.1 Trillion (2025)
- **Hiring Velocity**: High

---

## Technical Stack Detectors
- **Frontend**: Angular, React, WebGL
- **Backend**: C++, Python, CUDA C
- **Databases**: Cassandra, PostgreSQL, Redis
- **Cloud Infrastructure**: Hybrid (AWS, GCP, Azure, and Custom DGX Cloud)

---

## SWOT Summary
- **Strengths**: Dominant market position in AI enterprise chips, high CUDA software ecosystem lock-in.
- **Weaknesses**: Supply chain single-point concentration (TSMC dependencies).
- **Opportunities**: Expansion of AI software stack solutions (NIMs) and industrial robotics platforms.
- **Threats**: Custom ASIC chip architectures developed by hyper-scalers (TPU, Trainium).
